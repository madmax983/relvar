## 2024-05-18 - Avoid cloning heading per tuple in ungroup
**Learning:** Sometimes the requested optimization (like eliminating an Arc clone) is structurally impossible without changing core architectural signatures (like `Tuple::new_unchecked` requiring owned Arcs). In such cases, look for other heavier operations happening in the same tight loop. In `ungroup`, constructing a `BTreeMap` and fetching/cloning the outer tuple's attributes in the `O(N*M)` inner loop was a massive hidden cost. Hoisting the invariant `BTreeMap` creation out of the inner loop and just using `.clone()` provides a solid performance boost without touching core types.
**Action:** Inlined `build_ungrouped_tuple` into `compute_ungrouped_tuples`, pre-computed a `base_values` BTreeMap containing the outer tuple's non-RVA attributes, and cloned that map inside the inner loop instead of rebuilding it from scratch.
