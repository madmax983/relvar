## 2024-05-18 - Extract logic from closures in relational algorithms
**Learning:** Found complex and deeply nested relational operators (Join/Extend/Restrict) in experimental modules (`circuit.rs`, `game_of_life.rs`, `physics.rs`, `automata.rs`) acting as God Functions, mainly due to massive inline closures (`|t| { ... }`). Also, testing files lacked DRY helpers.
**Action:** Extract large closures from `extend` or `restrict` into standalone helper functions (`compute_binary_gate`, `compute_fx`, `compute_nx`) and use `move |t| compute_fx(t, g)` to pass context cleanly. Extract boilerplate into factories (`create_empty_states`).
