🌟 Nova: Quantum Circuit Simulator

💡 **The Spark:** "I noticed we can use tuples to model basis states and complex amplitudes for a superposition. What if we use joins as tensor products and summarization as matrix multiplications to simulate a quantum system?"
🚀 **The Feature:** "Implemented `QuantumState` representing a quantum register's probability amplitudes, and a `hadamard_gate` builder. Applied single-qubit gates map smoothly to relational algebra."
🔮 **The Potential:** "Could be expanded to implement multi-qubit gates and full quantum algorithms like Grover's purely via Relational engines!"
⚠️ **Risk:** "Low. Isolated in `src/experimental/quantum.rs`."
